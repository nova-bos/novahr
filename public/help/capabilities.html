Full capability list

Everything below is live in NovaHR today.

Accounts, roles & access

  • Self-service company sign-up and free trial
  • Four roles: HR admin, Manager, Employee, Executive
  • Email invites, single or bulk, with resend and revoke
  • Two-factor authentication (authenticator app)
  • Multi-company: one account across many workspaces
  • Branch-scoped admins; per-user role changes

Employee management

  • Rich records: identity (SA ID or passport), contact, next-of-kin, address, tax number
  • Guided onboarding wizard and bulk CSV import
  • Qualifications, skills, languages and custom fields
  • Employment-equity demographics
  • Probation, history timeline, org chart and directory
  • Documents vault with version history

Leave management

  • Full BCEA leave framework, configurable per company
  • Accrual or upfront, carryover and caps, sick-note rules
  • Employer-paid family leave toggles
  • Day-picker with half-days and public-holiday awareness
  • Multi-reviewer approval, calendar, balances, history
  • Leave encashment during and on exit

Payroll

  • SA PAYE engine: monthly, fortnightly and weekly
  • UIF, SDL, medical-aid credit, pension and RA caps
  • Overtime, allowances, commission, bonus and back-pay
  • Recurring components and variable-pay CSV import
  • Pay groups by branch and frequency; off-cycle runs
  • Lock and publish; bank export and Netcash; GL journal

Statutory compliance

  • EMP201 monthly declaration
  • EMP501 reconciliation and IRP5 / IT3(a) certificates
  • ETI calculation and claim schedule
  • UIF declaration export
  • COIDA Return of Earnings (W.As.8)
  • Employment Equity EEA2 and EEA4, with PDF; POPIA support

Payslips & self-service

  • Branded payslip PDFs, single or bulk ZIP
  • Payslip studio for layout and branding
  • Employee self-service payslip and certificate download
  • Self-service profile, banking and documents

People operations

  • Performance reviews with ratings and goals
  • Disciplinary records with issue and expiry dates
  • Termination flow with final pay and leave encashment
  • Generated letters: offer, confirmation, warning, termination
  • Deductions and loans with recovery tracking

Reports & platform

  • Payroll, workforce, leave and equity reports with charts
  • CSV and Excel exports throughout
  • Multi-tenant with app-layer isolation and rate limiting
  • Secure hosting, audit-minded design, email notifications

Limitations & what is not yet included

We would rather set expectations clearly. These are the current boundaries.

AreaPlease note
Statutory formsCOIDA (Return of Earnings) and Employment Equity outputs are prepared for you but carry a reminder to verify against the current Department of Labour or Compensation Fund form before you submit.
Direct e-filingNovaHR produces SARS-ready declarations and certificates (EMP201, EMP501, IRP5/IT3a). It does not file directly to SARS eFiling on your behalf; you upload or capture the prepared outputs.
Bank paymentsPayroll produces EFT and Netcash batch files. NovaHR does not move money itself; payment is completed in your bank or Netcash.
Mobile appNovaHR runs in the web browser and works on mobile screens. There is no separate native iOS or Android app at this time.
Two-factor enforcementTwo-factor authentication is available to every user. Company-wide enforcement is a platform setting rather than an in-app toggle today.
Regional scopeNovaHR is built specifically for South African payroll and labour law. It is not configured for other countries' tax regimes.
Modules on the roadmapApplicant tracking and recruiting, deeper performance-management cycles, and a native mobile app are planned rather than shipped.
Tax accuracy. Tax tables and rates are pre-loaded and validated against SARS, and are not meant to be edited casually. Always confirm final figures with your accountant or tax practitioner before filing.
