# New Employee Onboarding Checklist (South Africa)

**Lead magnet: branded PDF. Footer: "Compiled by NovaHR (novahr.co.za). General information, not legal advice."**

---

## Before Day One

### Paperwork
- [ ] Signed employment contract (before starting: BCEA s 29 written particulars are mandatory);
- [ ] Job description agreed and attached;
- [ ] Certified copy of ID or work permit (verify right to work);
- [ ] SARS income tax number obtained (employee can register on eFiling if new);
- [ ] Bank account confirmation letter;
- [ ] Medical aid and retirement fund enrolment forms, if offered;
- [ ] POPIA privacy notice given: what personal data you hold and why.

### Payroll setup
- [ ] Employee captured in payroll: exact date of birth (drives tax rebates), salary, allowances, deductions;
- [ ] Medical aid dependants recorded (drives tax credits);
- [ ] UIF declaration updated (UI-19: new starter);
- [ ] Leave balances initialised (annual accrual starts day one; sick leave: 1 day per 26 worked for the first 6 months);
- [ ] Pay date and payslip delivery explained.

### Practical
- [ ] Workspace, tools, uniform, and system accounts ready;
- [ ] Access cards/keys arranged;
- [ ] Team told a new person starts (name, role, start date).

## Day One

- [ ] Welcome and workplace tour (include emergency exits: OHSA);
- [ ] Introductions to team and direct manager;
- [ ] Company policies walked through and acknowledged: hours, leave process, disciplinary code, IT/social media use;
- [ ] Health and safety induction;
- [ ] Self-service access set up (payslips, leave requests);
- [ ] First-week plan handed over: who trains them on what.

## First Week

- [ ] Role training underway against the job description;
- [ ] Probation terms and review dates confirmed in writing;
- [ ] Buddy or go-to person assigned;
- [ ] Check-in at end of week: questions, blockers, impressions.

## First Month

- [ ] First payslip correct? (New starters are where payroll errors live: check tax, UIF, pro-rata);
- [ ] 30-day review against probation goals, documented;
- [ ] Outstanding paperwork chased to zero;
- [ ] Feedback both ways: what would have made onboarding better?
