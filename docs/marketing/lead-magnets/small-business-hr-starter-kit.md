# The Small Business HR Starter Kit (Bundle)

**The flagship lead magnet: a zip/single PDF bundling the five individual magnets, gated behind email sign-up. Highest perceived value; promote on the website footer, LinkedIn, and the email signature.**

---

## What the Kit Contains

1. **HR Compliance Checklist:** every registration, record, and deadline for SA employers (`hr-compliance-checklist.md`);
2. **Payroll Calendar 2026/27:** the printable one-pager with every SARS date (`payroll-calendar.md`);
3. **Employee Onboarding Checklist:** paperwork to first-month review (`employee-onboarding-checklist.md`);
4. **Leave Policy Template:** BCEA-aligned, fill-in-the-brackets (.docx) (`leave-policy-template.md`);
5. **Performance Review Template:** the one-page SME review (.docx) (`performance-review-template.md`);
6. **HR Policy Handbook Template:** ten policies, ready to adapt (.docx) (`hr-policy-template.md`).

## Landing Page Copy

**Headline:** Run HR like a real company, without hiring an HR department.

**Sub:** Six practical, South African templates and checklists: BCEA-aligned, SARS-deadline-aware, written for owners, not lawyers. Free.

**Bullets:**
- Know every SARS and labour deadline for the year;
- Give every new hire a professional, compliant start;
- Put a real leave policy and handbook in place this week;
- Templates in editable format: add your logo and go.

**Form:** Name, email, company size dropdown (1-10 / 11-30 / 31+; feeds plan targeting). Checkbox (unticked): "Send me monthly SA payroll deadline reminders and HR tips."

**After download (email automation):**
- Day 0: kit delivery + one-line intro to NovaHR;
- Day 3: "The 3 payroll fields that cause most payslip errors" (educational);
- Day 7: payroll calendar highlight + EMP201 reminder offer (WhatsApp channel);
- Day 14: soft product email: ROI framing + trial link;
- Then: monthly newsletter only. Unsubscribe honoured everywhere (POPIA s 69).
