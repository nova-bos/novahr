## Part 1: Preparation (both complete before the meeting)

### Employee self-review (send 1 week ahead)

1. What are you most proud of this period?
2. What did not go as planned, and why?
3. Where do you need more support, tools, or training?
4. What do you want to be doing more of next year?

### Manager preparation

1. Results against the goals set last review (be specific: numbers, examples);
2. Two strengths shown this period (with examples);
3. One or two growth areas (behaviours, not personality);
4. Any customer, peer, or quality feedback worth reflecting.

## Part 2: Ratings (keep it simple)

*Rule: no rating without an example. "Meets expectations" is a good, honest rating.*

## Part 3: The Conversation (agenda, 45-60 min)

1. Employee's self-review first (10 min: listen);
2. Manager's assessment, strengths before growth areas (15 min);
3. Discuss gaps between the two views openly (10 min);
4. Agree goals and development actions (15 min);
5. Anything else on the employee's mind (5 min).
