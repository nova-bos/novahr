# [COMPANY NAME] Leave Policy

**Effective date:** [insert effective date] | **Applies to:** All employees | **Owner:** [HR Manager / Business Owner]

## 1. General

1.1 This policy sets out leave entitlements, which meet or exceed the Basic Conditions of Employment Act 75 of 1997 ("BCEA"). Where an employment contract grants more, the contract applies.

1.2 The leave year runs [from employment anniversary / 1 January to 31 December: choose].

1.3 All leave must be requested via [NovaHR / the leave system] and approved by [manager] before being taken, except emergencies, where notice must be given as soon as possible.

## 2. Annual Leave

2.1 Entitlement: **[15] working days** per leave cycle for a 5-day week ([18] for a 6-day week), accruing monthly ([1.25] days per month).

2.2 Requests: at least [2 weeks] in advance; approval considers operational needs; December shutdown [does / does not] apply: [detail].

2.3 Carry-over: up to [5] days may carry into the next cycle and must be used within [6] months (BCEA requires leave be granted within 6 months of cycle end). No cash-in-lieu except on termination.

2.4 Public holidays during annual leave are not counted as leave days.

## 3. Sick Leave

3.1 Entitlement: **[30] days per 36-month cycle** (5-day week). During the first 6 months of employment: 1 day per 26 days worked.

3.2 A medical certificate is required for more than 2 consecutive days, or on a pattern of absence [define: e.g. more than twice in 8 weeks] (BCEA s 23).

3.3 Notify [manager] by [09:00] on the first day of absence.

## 4. Family Responsibility Leave

**[3] days** per year (employees employed 4+ months, working 4+ days/week) for: birth or illness of the employee's child, or death of a spouse/life partner, parent, adoptive parent, grandparent, child, adopted child, grandchild, or sibling. Proof may be requested. Unused days do not carry over.

## 5. Maternity, Parental, and Adoption Leave

5.1 **Maternity:** 4 consecutive months. Pay: [unpaid / % topped up: ●]; employees may claim UIF maternity benefits; the company will provide required documentation promptly. Notice: [4 weeks] before intended start.

5.2 **Parental:** 10 consecutive days from birth/adoption. Pay: [unpaid; UIF claimable / paid: ●].

5.3 **Adoption / commissioning parental:** 10 weeks / 10 days respectively, per BCEA ss 25B-25C.

## 6. Unpaid Leave

Granted at the company's discretion once paid entitlements are exhausted. Deducted from salary at the daily rate. Affects [bonus accrual / leave accrual: define].

## 7. Study Leave [optional]

[enter number] days per year for approved courses or exams, with proof of registration. The Company reserves the right to require repayment if the employee leaves within 12 months of completing the course.

## 8. Religious and Other Leave [optional]

[Company position: typically annual leave or unpaid leave by arrangement.]

## 9. Records and Administration

Balances are visible in [NovaHR self-service]. Leave records are retained for at least 3 years (BCEA s 31). On termination, accrued untaken annual leave is paid out (BCEA s 40).

## 10. Abuse

Dishonest leave claims (fraudulent sick notes, working elsewhere while on sick leave) are misconduct handled under the disciplinary code.
