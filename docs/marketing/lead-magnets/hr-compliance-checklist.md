## How to Use This

Work through each section once, then re-run the "Monthly" and "Annual" sections on schedule. If you tick every box, you are ahead of most SMEs in the country.

## 1. When You Hire Your First Employee

- [ ] Register as an employer with SARS for PAYE (within 21 business days of becoming an employer);
- [ ] Register with the UIF (Department of Employment and Labour / uFiling);
- [ ] Register with the Compensation Fund (COIDA) and budget for the annual Return of Earnings;
- [ ] Written employment contract signed before day one (BCEA s 29 written particulars);
- [ ] Capture the employee's income tax number, ID, and bank details securely (POPIA applies to you now);
- [ ] Display the required BCEA and OHSA summaries at the workplace.

## 2. Every Employee File Must Contain

- [ ] Signed employment contract and job description;
- [ ] Certified ID copy;
- [ ] SARS tax number;
- [ ] Banking confirmation;
- [ ] Leave records (BCEA: keep 3 years);
- [ ] Payslip history (SARS: keep 5 years);
- [ ] Disciplinary records, if any.

## 3. Payroll: Every Month

- [ ] Payslips issued with the BCEA s 33 particulars (employer details, period, gross, each deduction named, net);
- [ ] PAYE calculated on current-year SARS tables (they change every March);
- [ ] UIF: 1% employee + 1% employer, up to the ceiling;
- [ ] SDL: 1% if your annual payroll exceeds R500,000;
- [ ] **EMP201 filed and paid by the 7th** of the following month;
- [ ] UI-19 declaration submitted (new hires and terminations especially);
- [ ] Payroll reports archived.

## 4. Leave: Check Your Policy Grants at Least

- [ ] Annual leave: 21 consecutive days per cycle (15 working days for a 5-day week);
- [ ] Sick leave: 30 days per 36-month cycle (5-day week), with the first-6-months accrual rule;
- [ ] Family responsibility: 3 days/year (qualifying employees);
- [ ] Maternity: 4 months; parental: 10 days;
- [ ] Public holidays paid and not deducted from annual leave.

## 5. Annual and Periodic

- [ ] EMP501 reconciliations (interim and annual windows per SARS notice);
- [ ] IRP5s issued to all employees after annual reconciliation;
- [ ] COIDA Return of Earnings filed;
- [ ] National minimum wage compliance re-checked after each yearly adjustment;
- [ ] Employment contracts reviewed against any new sectoral determination;
- [ ] If 50+ employees: Employment Equity plan and reporting.

## 6. POPIA Quick Check (you hold employee data)

- [ ] Employee data stored securely (not an unprotected spreadsheet emailed around);
- [ ] Access limited to people who need it;
- [ ] An Information Officer registered with the Information Regulator;
- [ ] A privacy notice given to employees about how their data is used;
- [ ] Any payroll provider or software vendor bound by a written agreement (POPIA s 20-21 requires it).
