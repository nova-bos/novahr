# [COMPANY NAME] Employee Handbook

**Effective:** [●] | **Version:** 1.0 | **Owner:** [●]

*This handbook summarises company policies. It does not replace employment contracts. Where law grants more than this handbook, the law applies.*

## 1. Working Hours and Attendance

Ordinary hours: [08:00-17:00, Monday-Friday, 1 hour unpaid lunch] (max 45/week per BCEA). Attendance is recorded via [NovaHR / clock system]. Late arrival or absence: notify [manager] by [time]. Repeated unexplained absence is misconduct.

## 2. Remuneration and Payslips

Pay day: [25th]. Payslips are issued via [self-service/email] each pay day showing gross, deductions, and net (BCEA s 33). Queries to [●] within 5 days of pay day. Salary reviews: [annually in ●, not guaranteed].

## 3. Overtime

Overtime is voluntary-by-agreement, pre-approved by [manager], and paid at 1.5x (2x Sundays/public holidays) or time off by agreement, per BCEA. Employees earning above the BCEA threshold: overtime terms per contract.

## 4. Leave

Per the standalone Leave Policy: annual [15 days], sick [30/36-month cycle], family responsibility [3], maternity [4 months], parental [10 days]. All leave via [NovaHR].

## 5. Code of Conduct

We expect honesty, respect, punctuality, and care with company property and money. Zero tolerance: theft, fraud, violence, harassment, intoxication at work, and discrimination (Employment Equity Act). Dress: [●]. Conflicts of interest must be declared.

## 6. Disciplinary Procedure (summary)

Progressive discipline: verbal warning, written warning, final written warning, hearing, dismissal, matched to severity (serious misconduct may skip steps). Employees are entitled to state their case and be assisted by a colleague at a hearing (LRA Schedule 8). Warnings lapse after [6/12] months. Full code: [annexure].

## 7. Grievances

Raise first with your manager; unresolved within [5] days, escalate in writing to [owner/HR]. No retaliation for good-faith grievances.

## 8. IT, Communication, and Social Media

Company systems are for business use (reasonable personal use tolerated). No sharing of confidential or personal information (POPIA), no offensive content, no speaking for the company on social media unless authorised. Company may monitor company systems to the extent lawful and disclosed.

## 9. Protection of Personal Information

We collect and process employee information for employment, payroll, and legal purposes, per our POPIA privacy notice [annexure]. Employees must likewise protect any customer or colleague data they handle. Breaches: report immediately to [Information Officer].

## 10. Health and Safety

Comply with the Occupational Health and Safety Act and site rules: report hazards and incidents to [●] immediately. First aid: [location/officer]. Emergency assembly: [●].

## 11. Termination

Notice periods (or contract, if longer): 1 week (employed under 6 months), 2 weeks (6-12 months), 4 weeks (1 year+). Final pay includes accrued leave (BCEA s 40). Company property returned on last day; UI-19 and certificate of service provided.
