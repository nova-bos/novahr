## Every Single Month

| Date | Deadline |
|---|---|
| **7th** | EMP201: pay and file PAYE + UIF + SDL for last month (late = automatic 10% penalty) |
| **7th** | UIF payment (with EMP201, or direct to the Fund) |
| Month-end | UI-19 employee declarations (uFiling), especially starters and leavers |
| Your pay day | Payslips issued with BCEA-required details |

## The 2026/27 Tax Year at a Glance

| Month | What is due |
|---|---|
| **March 2026** | New tax year begins on the 1st. New PAYE tables apply to your first March payroll. Check your software updated |
| **April 2026** | COIDA Return of Earnings window typically opens; SETA Workplace Skills Plan due 30 April (SDL grant claims) |
| **April-May 2026** | **EMP501 annual reconciliation** (window per SARS notice); then issue **IRP5s** to every employee |
| **June 2026** | Quiet month: good time to audit employee files and leave balances |
| **August 2026** | Interim period ends 31 August |
| **Sept-Oct 2026** | **EMP501 interim reconciliation** (window per SARS notice) |
| **September 2026** | Employment Equity online reporting opens (designated employers), runs to 15 January |
| **December 2026** | Annual shutdown? Confirm leave treatment and December pay date early; December pay often runs early, EMP201 deadline does not move |
| **January 2027** | 15 January: EE reporting closes (online). Everyone back: process December leave |
| **February 2027** | Tax year ends the 28th. Budget speech: watch for new tables |

## Public Holidays 2026 (payroll-relevant)

1 Jan, 21 Mar (falls Sat), 3 Apr (Good Friday), 6 Apr (Family Day), 27 Apr, 1 May, 16 Jun, 9 Aug (falls Sun: 10 Aug holiday), 24 Sep, 16 Dec, 25 Dec, 26 Dec (falls Sat).
*Work on a public holiday = at least double pay (BCEA s 18). Verify each year's Sunday shifts and any proclaimed extras.*

## The Five Numbers to Memorise (2026/27)

| | |
|---|---|
| UIF | 1% + 1%, ceiling R17,712/month (max R177.12 each) |
| SDL | 1% if annual payroll > R500,000 |
| Tax threshold (under 65) | R99,000/year |
| Medical tax credit | R376 + R376 + R254/month |
| EMP201 day | The 7th. Always the 7th |
