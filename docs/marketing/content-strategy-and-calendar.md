# NovaHR Content Strategy and Calendar

**Owner:** Founder
**Cadence:** sustainable for one person: 3 social posts/week, 1 LinkedIn article/month, 1 lead magnet/quarter.
**Positioning:** the practical compliance guide for SA small business owners. Teach PAYE/BCEA/POPIA in plain language; the product appears as the natural way to apply the lesson.

---

## 1. Pillars (rotate through the week)

| Pillar | Share | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Compliance education** | 40% | "EMP201 is due Monday: the 60-second checklist"; "What BCEA actually says about sick notes"; "POPIA and the salary spreadsheet on your laptop" |
| **Payroll and HR tips** | 30% | "The 3 fields that break payslips (DOB, dependants, travel %)"; "How to read your PAYE"; leave balance myths |
| **Product** | 20% | Feature clips (15-30s screen recordings), release notes highlights, "how X works in NovaHR" |
| **Founder/behind the scenes** | 10% | Build-in-public updates, why decisions were made, SA small business commentary |

## 2. Channels

- **LinkedIn (primary):** owners, accountants, HR managers live here. Posts + monthly article + comments on accounting/HR conversations;
- **Facebook business groups:** SA small business groups allow helpful content; never bare links;
- **WhatsApp Channel [to be set]:** deadline reminders (EMP201 on the 5th of each month performs reliably);
- **Blog (novahr.co.za/blog):** the long-form home; every article answers one search query ("how to calculate UIF 2026", "BCEA leave days South Africa"). SEO is the compounding channel;
- **Email list:** monthly newsletter: one deadline reminder, one tip, one product note. Built from lead magnets.

## 3. Recurring Calendar (evergreen skeleton)

| When | Content |
|---|---|
| 1st week of month | Deadline post: "EMP201 due the 7th" + tip |
| Mid-month | Educational deep-dive (pillar 1 or 2) + product clip |
| Month-end | "Before you run payroll this month" checklist post |
| February | Budget speech: what the new tax tables mean for payroll (biggest traffic moment of the year: prepare in advance, publish within 24h of the speech) |
| March | New tax year content blitz; "did your software update its tables?" |
| April/May | EMP501 season guides |
| August/September | Interim EMP501 reminders |
| December | Public holidays + annual shutdown leave rules |

## 4. Formats and Reuse (one effort, five assets)

Each monthly article decomposes into: 3 text posts, 1 infographic (Canva template), 1 short video/reel (talking head or screen capture), newsletter section, and an FAQ entry. Write the article first; slice after.

## 5. Rules

- Every compliance claim sourced (SARS, labour.gov.za, the Acts); date-stamp anything with figures;
- Always the disclaimer footer on tax/legal content: "General information, not tax or legal advice";
- No em dashes in any copy (brand writing rule);
- No engagement bait; answer real questions from support and sales calls (they are the content backlog);
- Product mentions earn their place: the lesson must stand alone without the pitch.

## 6. Measurement (monthly, 15 minutes)

Followers matter less than: profile-to-site clicks, lead magnet downloads, trial sign-ups attributing "saw your content", and search impressions on blog posts. Log in a simple sheet; double down on the pillar that drives trials.
