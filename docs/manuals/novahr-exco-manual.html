1. What the Executive role can do

Executive (Exco)

Your role in NovaHR

As an executive you have read-only access to all dashboards, reports, and compliance records in the company. You can see everything HR can see in terms of strategic metrics, but you cannot make any changes: no editing employees, no running payroll, no approving leave, and no changing settings. Your role is oversight and informed decision-making.

What you can see

  • Executive overview dashboard with all KPIs
  • Workforce reports: headcount, departments, types
  • Full payroll reports: cost trends, YTD totals, composition
  • Leave reports: usage by type across the company
  • Compliance status: PAYE, UIF, SDL return tracking
  • All employee profiles (read-only)
  • Public holiday calendar

What you cannot do

  • Edit any employee record
  • Approve or reject leave requests
  • Start or complete payroll runs
  • Change any company settings
  • Invite or remove users
  • Submit compliance returns
  • Export payroll data or payslips

2. Logging in

Go to novahr-five.vercel.app. You will see the login page if you are not already signed in.
Enter your email and password (set when you accepted the invitation from HR).
Click Sign in. You are taken to the executive dashboard with a full organisational overview.
🔐
Forgot your password? Click Forgot password on the login page to receive a reset email. If you never received an invitation, contact your HR administrator.

Workforce analytics

The Workforce report gives you a people-level view of your organisation:

  • Headcount trend: how many active employees over time, useful for tracking growth
  • Employment type split: full-time vs. part-time vs. contract, useful for capacity planning
  • Department breakdown: headcount per department with percentage of total

Payroll analytics

The Payroll report shows company payroll cost data:

MetricWhat it tells you
Monthly gross payroll trendHow total payroll cost has moved over the last 7 months
Net vs. PAYE vs. UIF chartBreakdown of where the gross goes: employee net, tax, and levies
Year-to-date totalCumulative payroll spend for the current tax year
📌
You can see total payroll cost but not individual employee salaries from the Reports view. To see individual salaries you would need to look at the Employees section and navigate to a specific person's compensation tab.

Leave analytics

The Leave report helps you spot patterns in how the team uses their leave:

  • Leave by type chart: how many approved days each leave type accounts for
  • Leave usage trend: approved leave days over time
  • High sick leave usage in a specific period may indicate a team-wide health issue or morale problem worth investigating

7. Viewing statutory compliance

PAYE, UIF, and SDL return status across all periods

1 2 3 4
1 PAYE liability for the current period (read-only)
2 UIF contributions for the current period
3 SDL levy for the current period
4 Filing history: status per period (Pending, Submitted, Accepted, Rejected)

The Compliance page shows you the statutory tax position for the company. As an executive this is read-only: you can see the amounts and filing status but cannot update them. Only HR administrators can record a submission or mark a return as accepted.

StatusWhat it means
PendingTax calculated but not yet filed with SARS
SubmittedFiled with SARS; awaiting confirmation
AcceptedSARS confirmed the return
RejectedSARS returned the filing; HR needs to re-file
⚠️
If you see returns stuck in Pending past the filing due date (7th of the following month), flag this to your HR administrator immediately. Late EMP201 filings attract SARS penalties.

8. Company-wide leave view

Go to Leave in the sidebar to see all leave requests across the company. As an executive you have a read-only view: you can see who has requested what and the approval status, but you cannot approve, reject, or submit leave requests.

💡
Use the Public holidays tab on the Leave page to see the full SA public holiday calendar for 2026, 2027, and 2028. This is useful for planning team availability and scheduling around national holidays.
