1. What the Manager role can do

Manager

Your role in NovaHR

As a manager you see your own full profile and the profiles of your direct reports. You can approve or reject leave requests from your team, view basic payroll cost summaries, and access workforce reports. You cannot see salaries, banking details, payslips, or PAYE details of people outside your team.

What you can do

  • View and manage your own profile and leave
  • See full profiles of all your direct reports
  • Approve or reject leave requests from your team
  • View team payroll cost (gross total, no individual salaries)
  • Submit leave on behalf of a team member
  • Access workforce and leave reports

Restrictions to be aware of

  • You cannot approve your own leave requests
  • You cannot see salaries of employees outside your team
  • You cannot access Payroll runs, Compliance, or Settings
  • You cannot see banking details of any employee
  • Changes to job titles or salaries must go through HR

2. Logging in

Go to novahr-five.vercel.app. You will see the login page if you are not already signed in.
Enter your email and password (the ones you set when you accepted your invitation from HR).
Click Sign in. You are taken directly to your manager dashboard.
🔐
Forgot your password? Click Forgot password on the login page. A reset email will be sent to the address HR registered for you.

NovaHRSection 4: Leave Approvals

6. Reviewing team leave requests

1 2 3 4
1 Request leave button: submit leave on behalf of a team member
2 Filter and tab navigation: Requests, Balances, Policies
3 Leave request list filtered to your team: employee, type, dates, working days
4 Quick approve (green tick) and reject (red X) buttons

Go to Leave in the sidebar. You only see leave requests from your direct reports (not the whole company). Requests are sorted with pending ones at the top.

What to check before approving

  • Is there enough leave balance remaining?
  • Will business operations be affected?
  • Are there other team members already on leave at the same time?
  • Has the employee provided a reason (required for all types)?
  • For sick leave over 2 days: is a medical certificate attached?

Leave type quick guide

  • Annual: pre-planned; requires reasonable notice
  • Sick: unexpected; may require medical certificate
  • Family: for immediate family events; 3 days/year
  • Maternity/parental: refer to HR
  • Study: exams or study; 5 days/year

7. Approving or rejecting a request

Find the pending request in the Leave page or on your dashboard's Leave approvals card. Pending requests have an orange badge.
To approve: click the green tick ✔ button on the right side of the request row. The decision is saved immediately, the employee's balance is decremented, and the employee receives an email notification.
To reject: click the red X button. A text field appears asking for a rejection reason. Enter a brief explanation (for example "Team is fully staffed this week; please re-request for the following week") and click Reject. The employee is notified of the rejection and the reason you provided.
⚠️
You cannot approve or reject your own leave requests. Your leave requests go directly to HR for approval. This is a system rule to prevent conflicts of interest.
✅
Once approved, the leave request status changes to Approved (green badge) and the employee's balance is automatically updated. You cannot undo an approval; if a change is needed, ask HR to adjust the balance manually.

8. Your own leave requests

Submitting leave as a manager works the same as for employees

Click Request leave at the top of the Leave page. You are submitting leave for yourself.
Select the leave type, dates, and add a reason. The working-day count is calculated automatically.
Submit the request. It goes to your HR administrator for approval. Your manager (if you have one set) may also be notified.
💡
Try to submit leave requests as early as possible, especially for annual leave, so HR can plan coverage for your team while you are away.

9. Team reports

Analytics for your team and the broader organisation

Go to Reports in the sidebar to access workforce and leave analytics.

Workforce report

  • Headcount trend over time
  • Employment type mix
  • Department breakdown across the company

Leave report

  • Approved leave days by type (company-wide)
  • Leave usage trend chart
📌
As a manager you see company-wide workforce and leave data in reports. Individual salary figures and payroll run details are not visible; only HR and executives with full access can see that information.

Leave approval best practices

  • ✔ Aim to respond to leave requests within 1 business day
  • ✔ Check the leave balance before approving to avoid negative balances
  • ✔ Always provide a clear reason when rejecting, and suggest an alternative date if possible
  • ✔ For medical certificates, check that a document is attached to sick leave requests before approving
  • ✔ If you are unsure about a leave type (for example maternity or commissioning parental leave), escalate to HR
  • ✔ Submit your own leave requests early to give HR adequate time to plan coverage
