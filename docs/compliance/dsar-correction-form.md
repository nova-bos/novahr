### For office use (NovaHR)

Per POPIA s 24, on receipt of a valid request we will, as soon as reasonably practicable: correct the information; or destroy or delete it; or provide the data subject with credible evidence in support of the information; or, where agreement cannot be reached, attach a note to the information indicating that a correction was requested but not made. We will notify the data subject of the action taken and, where reasonably practicable, notify third parties to whom the information was previously disclosed.
