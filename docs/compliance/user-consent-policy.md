# NovaHR User Consent Policy

**Version:** 1.0 (Draft, pending legal review)
**Effective Date:** 10 July 2026
**Audience:** Internal (product and engineering) and customer-facing summary

This policy defines when NovaHR relies on consent, how consent is captured, recorded, and withdrawn, and where other lawful bases apply instead.

---

## 1. Principles

1.1 Under POPIA, consent must be **voluntary, specific, and informed** (s 1 definition). We never bundle unrelated consents, and we never make core service access conditional on consent to marketing.

1.2 Consent is **not** our default lawful basis. Most processing in NovaHR rests on performance of a contract, legal obligation, or legitimate interest. We use consent only where the law requires it or no other basis fits.

## 2. Where Consent Is Used

| Processing | Basis | Consent mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Providing the platform to customers | Contract | None needed; Terms of Service acceptance |
| Processing employee data for payroll | Employer's obligation and contract of employment (employer is Responsible Party) | Employer warrants lawful basis in the DPA; NovaHR does not collect employee consent |
| Marketing emails to non-customers | Consent (POPIA s 69) | Explicit opt-in checkbox, unticked by default |
| Marketing to existing customers about similar services | s 69(3) soft opt-in | Opt-out offered at collection and in every message |
| Non-essential cookies / analytics with identifiers | Consent | Cookie banner, declined by default |
| Testimonials, logos, case studies | Consent | Written release signed by the customer |
| Product usage research interviews | Consent | Recorded verbal or written consent per session |

## 3. Capturing Terms and Policy Acceptance (in-app)

3.1 At first onboarding, the account owner must affirmatively accept: Terms of Service, Data Processing Agreement, and acknowledge the Privacy Policy. Acceptance is captured with: user ID, tenant ID, document version, timestamp (UTC), and IP address, stored in an immutable acceptance log.

3.2 When a legal document changes materially, users are shown the new version on next login and must re-accept; the prior acceptance record is retained.

3.3 Invited users (employees, managers) accept the Terms of Service and acknowledge the Privacy Policy at first login.

## 4. Withdrawal of Consent

4.1 Marketing consent can be withdrawn via the unsubscribe link in any message or by emailing hello@novahr.co.za; effect within 7 days, without affecting service delivery.

4.2 Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal, and does not override statutory retention duties.

## 5. Records

The Information Officer maintains a consent register covering: what was consented to, the wording shown, version, date, channel, and withdrawal history. Acceptance logs in the application form part of this register.

## 6. Children

NovaHR does not knowingly seek consent from children. Employee records of minors (e.g. learnerships) are processed by the employer under employment law, not consent collected by NovaHR.

## 7. Review

Reviewed annually by the Information Officer and whenever a new consent-based processing activity is proposed. New consent flows require Information Officer sign-off before release.
