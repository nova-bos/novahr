- **Monthly:** UI-19 declarations to the Department of Employment and Labour;
- **March:** new tax year; NovaHR updates tables automatically;
- **April/May and Sept/Oct:** EMP501 reconciliations;

## Page 6: Five Tips From Us

1. Verify dates of birth and medical dependants before your first run;
2. Never share logins; invite each person with their own role;
3. Export and archive every payroll run report (SARS: 5 years);
4. Use the parallel run for your first month; it catches setup gaps;
5. Email us feature requests; the roadmap is shaped by customers.
