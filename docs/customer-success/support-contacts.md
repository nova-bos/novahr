## Contact Matrix

| Situation | Contact | Response target |
|---|---|---|
| General questions, how-to | hello@novahr.co.za | 1 business day |
| Bug or error | hello@novahr.co.za (subject: BUG) | Per severity (Support Policy) |
| **Payroll blocked on pay day** | hello@novahr.co.za (subject: **P1**) [+ WhatsApp ●: Growth/Scale] | 2 business hours |

| Data subject / POPIA requests | hello@novahr.co.za (attention: Information Officer) | Acknowledged in 3 business days |
| Escalation | Reply with ESCALATE in subject, or [founder email ●] | Same business day |

**Business hours:** 08:00-17:00 SAST, Monday to Friday, excluding SA public holidays. Priority support hours (Growth/Scale): 07:00-19:00 for P1.

## What to Include for Fast Resolution

1. Company name and your account email;
2. What you were doing, what you expected, what happened;
3. Module and page (e.g. Payroll, run for June);
4. Screenshot with ID numbers and bank details redacted;
5. "P1" in the subject if a pay-day payroll run is blocked.
