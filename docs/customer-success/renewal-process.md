# NovaHR Renewal Process

**Owner:** Founder / customer success
**Applies to:** annual plans and signed MSA customers (monthly self-serve renews automatically with no process).

---

## Timeline (annual renewals)

| When | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| T-90 days | Health check: usage, tickets, plan fit. Flag at-risk accounts and open a save plan | CS |
| T-60 days | Renewal notice with pricing for the new term (60 days required for price changes on annual plans, per Subscription and Payment Terms) | CS |
| T-45 days | Renewal conversation for Scale/MSA customers: review the year, roadmap preview, plan fit, upsell if employee count grew | Founder |
| T-30 days | Contractual non-renewal notice deadline (per MSA clause 5.1); confirm intent in writing | Customer/CS |
| T-14 days | Issue renewal Order Form or confirm auto-renewal; new invoice scheduled | CS |
| T-0 | Renewal effective; thank-you note; update CRM and Order records | CS |

## At-Risk Playbook

Trigger: Red/Amber health score, declining logins, missed payroll runs, unresolved complaints, or an explicit cancellation signal.

1. Diagnose from data before calling: what stopped, when, after what event;
2. Call the account owner; listen first; log the root cause (product gap, service failure, business change, price);
3. Offer the right lever, in this order: fix the actual problem, training session, plan downgrade, [discount only with founder approval, max ●%];
4. If leaving: execute the Offboarding Checklist gracefully; a clean exit preserves referrals and the door back.

## Rules

- No auto-renewal surprises: every annual customer hears from us at T-60 at the latest;
- Price increases: never applied mid-term; 60 days' notice; grandfather where the relationship justifies it;
- Churn reasons are logged verbatim and reviewed monthly; they feed the roadmap.
