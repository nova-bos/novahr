# NovaHR Training Schedule Template

**Used by:** Customer success when onboarding Growth/Scale customers; adaptable for self-serve customers as a self-paced plan.
**Delivery:** video calls (45-60 min each) plus self-paced guides and videos.

---

## Session Plan

### Session 1: Administrator Foundation (Week 1, 60 min)
**Audience:** HR Admins
**Agenda:**
1. Platform tour: navigation, dashboard, roles (10 min)
2. Company settings and statutory configuration (15 min)
3. Departments, positions, and employee capture (20 min)
4. Q&A and homework: capture 3 real employees (15 min)

**Materials:** Quick Start Guide, Payroll Setup Guide, Adding Employees video.

### Session 2: Payroll (Week 2, 60 min)
**Audience:** HR Admins, finance
**Agenda:**
1. Anatomy of a payslip: gross to net, PAYE, UIF, SDL (15 min)
2. Running, reviewing, approving, publishing (20 min)
3. Once-off items: overtime, bonuses, unpaid leave (10 min)
4. Reports and EMP201 figures (10 min)
5. Q&A (5 min)

**Materials:** How-to: Process Payroll, Payroll Calculations and Auditing doc, go-live checklist.

### Session 3: Leave and Self-Service (Week 2-3, 45 min)
**Audience:** HR Admins and Managers
**Agenda:**
1. Leave policies and BCEA defaults (10 min)
2. Request and approval flows; team calendar (15 min)
3. Employee self-service walkthrough: payslips, requests, profile (10 min)
4. Rolling out to employees: invites and comms (10 min)

**Materials:** How-to: Approve Leave, Employee Manual (PDF), invite email template.

### Session 4: Reports, Audit, and Admin Hygiene (Week 3-4, 45 min)
**Audience:** HR Admins, Executives
**Agenda:**
1. Reports library and exports (15 min)
2. Audit log: what is recorded, how to review (10 min)
3. User management, deactivation, password resets (10 min)
4. Statutory calendar and monthly rhythm (10 min)

**Materials:** How-to: Export Reports, SARS Compliance Calendar, Audit Log Policy summary.

## Employee Rollout (customer-run, 15 min per group)

Short session or recorded video for all staff: how to log in, view payslips, request leave, update contact details, who to ask for help. Materials: Employee Manual, self-service video.
