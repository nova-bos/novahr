# NovaHR Support Ticket Guide

**Audience:** customers. How to raise a ticket, what happens next, and how to escalate.

---

## 1. Before You Raise a Ticket

Check, in order (60 seconds):

1. The FAQ and how-to guides (in-app Help): most "how do I" questions are answered there;
2. Release notes: the behaviour may be a recent, announced change;
3. [Status page ●]: if the whole app is down, we likely already know and are posting updates.

## 2. Raising a Ticket

Email **hello@novahr.co.za**. Subject prefixes route your ticket correctly:

| Prefix | Use for |
|---|---|
| (none) | Questions, how-to |
| BUG | Something is broken |
| **P1** | **Payroll blocked on/near pay day, or you cannot access the system at all** |
| BILLING | Invoices, plan changes |
| SECURITY | Suspected breach or security concern |
| FEATURE REQUEST | Ideas and improvements |

Include: company name, account email, module/page, steps, expected vs actual, redacted screenshot. The Bug Report Form shows the ideal structure.

## 3. What Happens Next

1. **Acknowledgement** with a ticket reference, within the response time for the severity (SLA: P1 2 business hours, P2 4 hours, P3 1 day, P4 2 days);
2. **Triage:** we reproduce the issue and confirm severity; we may ask follow-up questions (faster if the original report is complete);
3. **Updates** at the cadence in the SLA until resolved;
4. **Resolution:** we confirm the fix with you before closing; bugs get a regression test so they stay fixed.

## 4. Severity Definitions (summary)

- **P1:** service down, data at risk, or payroll blocked on pay day;
- **P2:** core feature broken, no workaround;
- **P3:** degraded, workaround exists;
- **P4:** cosmetic, questions, requests.

We set severity based on impact; tell us explicitly if a pay day is at stake, that always raises priority.

## 5. Escalation

Not getting traction? Reply on the same thread with **ESCALATE** in the subject. It goes to the founder directly. Please keep one thread per issue: history in one place resolves faster.

## 6. Our Commitments to You

- No ticket goes unanswered;
- We do not close tickets you have not confirmed resolved (auto-close only after 5 business days of silence, with a warning);
- Calculation-related reports are treated at elevated severity per our Incident Response Plan;
- We tell you honestly when something is a limitation or a roadmap item, not a bug.
