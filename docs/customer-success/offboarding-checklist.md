# NovaHR Customer Offboarding Checklist

**Owner:** Customer success
**Trigger:** cancellation notice received or subscription lapsed (90+ days unpaid).
**Principle:** a graceful exit. Departing customers are future referrals and boomerangs; and we have legal duties (export window, deletion, POPIA).

**Customer:** ______________ **Cancellation effective:** ______ **Export window ends:** ______

---

## 1. On Cancellation Notice

- [ ] Acknowledge in writing within 1 business day; confirm effective date per the Refund and Cancellation Policy (monthly: end of billing month; annual: end of term)
- [ ] Exit survey sent (see Exit Survey)
- [ ] Save attempt only if appropriate (see Renewal Process at-risk playbook); never obstruct a decided customer
- [ ] Churn reason logged in the register
- [ ] Final invoice or refund position confirmed (14-day guarantee / pro-rata rules)

## 2. Export Window (30 days from effective date)

- [ ] Account switched to read-only on effective date
- [ ] Export instructions email sent day 1: what to export (payroll run reports, payslips PDFs, employee register, leave records) and the statutory reminder (SARS 5 years, BCEA 3 years)
- [ ] Reminder day 7
- [ ] Final reminder day 23
- [ ] Export assistance offered for bulk data

## 3. Closure (after day 30)

- [ ] Access revoked; users deactivated
- [ ] Deletion job executed within 60 days (Data Deletion Policy section 4); deletion log entry recorded
- [ ] Backup purge confirmed after rotation window
- [ ] Deletion confirmation sent to the customer in writing
- [ ] Billing stopped and account closed in accounting system
- [ ] CRM updated: churned, reason, win-back date (+6 months)

## 4. Legal and Records

- [ ] Records NovaHR must retain preserved per Data Retention Policy (contracts, invoices, DSAR history): retention is limited to these, nothing else
- [ ] Any outstanding DSAR or dispute resolved before deletion, or legal hold applied
- [ ] DPA obligations on termination met (clause 7)

## 5. Learn

- [ ] Exit survey response reviewed
- [ ] Churn reason discussed in monthly review; product or service action created if warranted
- [ ] Win-back note scheduled if the reason was a missing feature we later ship
