## NovaHR
### South African HR and payroll software that small businesses can actually run themselves.

**The problem:** SA SMEs juggle spreadsheets, a payroll bureau invoice per payslip, paper leave forms, and SARS deadlines, with employee ID numbers and salaries scattered across all of them.

**NovaHR** puts it in one secure place: employee records, SARS-compliant payroll, payslips, leave, attendance, and reports. Built for South Africa, not adapted to it.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Payroll that knows SA law** | PAYE (2026/27 tables), UIF with the ceiling, SDL, medical tax credits, pension s11F caps, calculated automatically and verified against SARS sources |
| **Payslips in one click** | BCEA-compliant PDF payslips, published to employee self-service and email |
| **Leave without paper** | BCEA-default policies, request and approval flows, balances and team calendar |
| **Employee self-service** | Staff view payslips and request leave themselves; you stop being the print shop |
