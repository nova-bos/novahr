# NovaHR Outreach Templates

**Channels:** cold email, follow-up sequences, LinkedIn, WhatsApp.
**Legal:** POPIA s 69 and CPA govern electronic direct marketing. Cold B2B email to a business contact about a relevant business service is defensible practice, but every message must identify us, be honest, and offer a working opt-out honoured immediately. No purchased consumer lists.

---

## 1. Cold Email Sequence (3 touches, then stop)

### Email 1: the trigger

**Subject:** Payroll for [Company]'s [N] staff

> Hi [First name],
>
> [Personalised opener: "Saw you are hiring two more technicians" / "Congrats on the new Paarl branch".]
>
> Most [industry] businesses your size run payroll through a bureau or spreadsheets: fine until a payslip is wrong, a SARS deadline slips, or the one person who understands the spreadsheet goes on leave.
>
> NovaHR is South African software that does payroll (PAYE, UIF, SDL, payslips), leave, and employee records in one place, from R499 a month flat, no per-payslip fees.
>
> Worth a 20-minute look? I will show you a payslip calculated for a [industry] employee like yours, and if it does not fit, I will say so.
>
> [Name], founder, NovaHR
> hello@novahr.co.za | [site]
> *Reply "no thanks" and I will not email again.*

**Subject:** Closing the loop

## 2. Warm Follow-Ups

### After a demo (same day)

**Subject:** Your NovaHR numbers + next step

> Great speaking today, [First name]. As promised:
>
> - Your estimated saving: R[●]/month vs [current setup] (worksheet attached);
> - The [feature] you asked about: [answer/screenshot];
> - Proposal attached, valid 30 days.
>
> Suggested next step: start the trial and book your setup session here: [link]. Anything unclear, reply or call me on [number].

### Proposal chaser (+5 days)

> Hi [First name], checking the proposal did not get buried. Any questions I can answer, or would a short call with [accountant/partner] help? If the timing is wrong, tell me when to come back and I will diarise it.

### Gone-quiet (+14 days)

> Hi [First name], no pressure: has this dropped down the list, or did something in the proposal not sit right? Both are useful for me to know. If it is dead, say the word and I will close the file.

## 3. LinkedIn Templates

### Connection request (300 chars max)

> Hi [First name], I build NovaHR, SA payroll/HR software for businesses like [Company]. Not a pitch, I share practical SARS/BCEA compliance content for [industry] owners and would like to connect.

### After acceptance (no pitch for 1-2 interactions, then)

> Thanks for connecting, [First name]. Quick question while I have you: how do you run payroll for the team at [Company]: bureau, Sage, or in-house? Asking because most [industry] owners I speak to are overpaying per payslip and I am testing that assumption.

### Content-led touch

> [First name], we just published a payroll calendar with every SARS deadline for the tax year: [link]. Most owners pin it above the printer. No strings.

## 4. WhatsApp Templates

**Rule:** WhatsApp only after they have engaged (replied, met, or opted in). Never cold WhatsApp from a scraped number.

### Demo confirmation

> Hi [First name], [Name] from NovaHR: confirming our 20-min call tomorrow at [time]. I will send a video link here. Anything specific you want me to prepare (e.g. an overtime payslip example)?

### Trial nudge

> Hi [First name], saw you started the NovaHR trial: nice. Fastest path: add 2-3 real employees and run a test payslip, takes ~15 min with this guide: [link]. Ping me here if anything snags.

### Pay-day support line (customers)

> This number is monitored for payroll-blocking issues on pay days (Growth/Scale plans). For everything else, hello@novahr.co.za gets you a tracked ticket and faster resolution.

## 5. List Hygiene

- Source contacts from public business info, referrals, and inbound only;
- Suppression list honoured across all channels forever;
- Every send logged (who, when, template) in the CRM;
- Max 3 cold touches, then 90 days minimum before any re-approach with new relevance.
