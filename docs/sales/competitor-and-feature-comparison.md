## 1. Landscape

SA SME payroll/HR options fall into four buckets:

1. **SA payroll SaaS:** SimplePay, PaySpace (Deel), Sage Business Cloud Payroll;
2. **Accounting suites with payroll add-ons:** Xero + SimplePay integration, QuickBooks partners;
3. **Bureaus/accountants:** outsourced, per-payslip pricing;
4. **Spreadsheets:** the real competitor for 1-15 employee businesses.

## 2. Positioning in One Line

**NovaHR is the all-in-one HR + payroll for SA SMEs at a flat price:** competitors are either payroll-only (SimplePay), enterprise-oriented (PaySpace, Sage), or manual (bureau, Excel).

## 4. Where We Win (lead with these)

- **Total cost at SME scale:** flat R499/R999 beats per-employee pricing as headcount grows within a band;
- **HR + payroll in one:** competitors need two products for leave/attendance + payroll;
- **Simplicity:** built for an owner or office manager, not a payroll professional;
- **Suite story:** POS, accounting, and CRM from the same family (Nova Business OS);
- **Local, personal support:** founder-led support vs ticket queues.
