# NovaHR Demo Checklist

**Purpose:** every demo runs on a clean, credible environment and lands the three moments that sell: a correct payslip, self-service on a phone, and the accountant-ready report.

---

## Before (day prior)

- [ ] Discovery notes reviewed: their headcount, industry, named pains, current tool;
- [ ] Demo tenant ready with **their-world data**: company in their industry, 5-8 employees with realistic SA names, salaries near their range, one employee with overtime, one on leave;
- [ ] No test junk visible ("asdf", "Test123"): the demo tenant is a showroom;
- [ ] One employee prepared matching their scenario (e.g. R18,500/month with medical aid + 2 dependants) so the payslip discussion is concrete;
- [ ] Payslip PDF opens correctly; email delivery to your own address tested;
- [ ] Phone/tablet at hand for the self-service moment;
- [ ] Backup: screenshots/recording in case of connectivity failure;
- [ ] Proposal skeleton pre-filled with their numbers (send within 48h);
- [ ] Video link sent; calendar confirmed morning-of.

## Demo Flow (10-12 minutes of product, max)

- [ ] **Frame (1 min):** "You said [pain 1] and [pain 2]; I will show you exactly those, nothing else."
- [ ] **Moment 1: the payslip (4 min).** Open the prepared employee, run the calculation, walk the payslip: gross, PAYE with the medical credit visible, UIF capped, net. Say: "These are the 2026/27 SARS tables, verified and tested; here is the same number on the SARS calculator if you want to check live."
- [ ] **Moment 2: self-service on the phone (3 min).** Log in as an employee on the phone, download a payslip, request leave; approve it from your laptop as the manager. "Your staff stop asking you for payslips."
- [ ] **Moment 3: the accountant's report (2 min).** Reports: the run summary with EMP201 figures. "This is what your accountant needs on the 7th, thirty seconds."
- [ ] **Their questions (remainder).** Answer from the app, not slides.

## Never in a Demo

- Settings tours, module-by-module walkthroughs, or "and another thing" features;
- Live data entry of long forms (pre-load instead);
- Guessing at answers: "I will confirm and include it in the proposal" beats a wrong answer;
- Disparaging their current tool (they chose it).

## Close (2 min)

- [ ] Trial close: "Does this solve [named pain]?"
- [ ] Propose the concrete next step: trial + booked setup session, dates offered;
- [ ] Confirm decision process and timeline;
- [ ] Agree when the proposal arrives (within 48h).

## After (same day)

- [ ] CRM updated: attendees, reactions, objections, committed next step with date;
- [ ] Follow-up email sent: recap, answers to open questions, proposal, trial link;
- [ ] Demo tenant reset for the next prospect;
- [ ] Any question you could not answer: resolved and fed into the Sales FAQ.
