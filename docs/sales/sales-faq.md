# NovaHR Sales FAQ (Internal)

**Audience:** anyone selling NovaHR. Answers to prospect questions, with the honest position where the product has limits. Public product FAQ: `docs/customer/faq.md`.

---

## Product Scope

**Q: Does NovaHR file EMP201/EMP501 with SARS?**
No. It produces the exact figures; the customer or their accountant files on eFiling. Position as "your accountant keeps control"; direct integration is roadmap [●].

**Q: Does it generate IRP5 certificates?**
Roadmap [●]. Until then, the accountant generates IRP5s from our reports at reconciliation time. Be upfront; do not sell around it to a customer whose deciding factor this is.

**Q: Can it import employees from Sage/SimplePay/Excel?**
Manual capture today; migration assistance on Growth/Scale. CSV import is roadmap [●]. For under ~30 employees, capture is a few hours with our checklist.

**Q: Multiple companies under one login?**
[● verify current multi-tenant switching support]. Accountants running multiple clients: partner conversation with the founder.

**Q: Which pay frequencies?**
Monthly, bi-weekly, weekly.

**Q: Overtime, commissions, bonuses?**
Yes: captured per run as earnings lines with correct tax treatment. Complex incentive schemes (shares, expat): refer to a practitioner, qualify honestly.

**Q: Does it do performance management / recruitment / training records?**
No. HR core + payroll + leave + attendance. Do not promise the rest; note requests in the feature register.

## Commercial

**Q: Discounts?**
List price is the price. Founder may approve: annual prepay incentive [●%], NPO [●%], partner/accountant programme [●]. Never discount to close a wobbling deal; fix the objection instead.
**Q: Contract lock-in?**
Monthly plans: none, cancel at month-end. Annual: 12 months, with the 14-day guarantee up front. MSA customers: negotiated initial term.

**Q: Who signs what?**
Self-serve: ToS + DPA accepted in-app. Larger deals: MSA + Order Form + DPA countersigned. Templates in `docs/legal/`.

**Q: POPIA due diligence questionnaires?**
Answer from the POPIA Compliance Statement, DPA, and security policies in `docs/compliance/`. Never improvise security answers; if unsure, take the question away.

## Competitive

**Q: "SimplePay is [their per-employee price] per employee and well known."**
Concede brand, win on flat pricing at their headcount + HR-in-one + support. Matrix: `competitor-and-feature-comparison.md`. Verify competitor pricing before quoting it.

**Q: "Our bureau does everything."**
Different product: outsourced labour vs software + control. ROI sheet with their real bureau invoice usually decides it. If they truly want zero involvement, disqualify kindly.

## Delivery Promises (what you may commit to)

| You may promise | You may NOT promise |
|---|---|
| Two-week onboarding path with parallel run | A specific go-live date without seeing their data readiness |
| SLA response times as published | Custom SLA terms (founder only) |
| March SARS updates included | Any unreleased feature by a date (roadmap language only: founder approves exceptions) |
| 14-day money-back, 30-day export window | Refunds outside the policy |
| DPA signature | Contract redlines (founder + attorney only) |

## Escalate to Founder

Deals over [insert plan price]/month, MSA redlines, security questionnaires with unfamiliar items, partner/reseller requests, media/analyst contact, anything smelling of government procurement (B-BBEE affidavit needed first: `docs/business/` [●]).
