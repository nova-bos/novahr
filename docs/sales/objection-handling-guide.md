# NovaHR Objection Handling Guide

**Method:** acknowledge, clarify, respond with evidence, confirm. Never argue; an objection is a request for confidence.

---

## Price

**"R999 a month is a lot for a small business."**
> "Compared to what, though? Your bureau is R[●] per payslip, so R[●] a month, and you still track leave on paper. Add the [●] hours of your own time and NovaHR is usually the cheaper option before we count the HR side. Let us put your real numbers in the ROI sheet, and if you are genuinely cheaper today, I will tell you to stay."

**"Competitor X is cheaper per month."**
> "Per employee, often, and check what happens at your headcount and what is excluded: leave, attendance, and self-service are add-ons or absent. Flat R999 for up to 30 employees, everything included, is the honest comparison. Here is the matrix, dated and sourced."

## Trust and Track Record

**"I have never heard of NovaHR."**
> "Fair, we are new and local. Three things instead of a brand name: the tax engine is verified line-by-line against SARS sources with 200+ automated tests and a practitioner's sign-off you can read; your first month runs in parallel with your current payroll so nothing is at risk; and there is a 14-day money-back guarantee. You also get the founder's email, not a call centre."

**"What if you shut down?"**
> "Your data is exportable at any time, in standard formats, and if the service ever wound down you would get a contractual export window; that is in the Terms, not a promise. Meanwhile it runs on the same infrastructure grade (Vercel, Supabase, SOC 2 providers) the big names use."

## Risk and Compliance

**"Payroll errors are catastrophic; I trust my bureau."**
> "Rightly so, that is why we insist on the parallel run: same month, both systems, reconcile to the cent before you switch. And unlike a bureau, every calculation is on your screen with the audit trail, not in someone else's system. Bring your accountant into the verification; we like that."

**"Is my employees' data safe? POPIA worries me."**
> "It should; spreadsheets on laptops are the actual risk. NovaHR encrypts data in transit and at rest, isolates every company at database level, logs every access to payroll, and we sign a Data Processing Agreement with you, which POPIA actually requires of any provider touching employee data. Ask your current provider for theirs."

**"You don't submit to eFiling like Sage does."**
> "True today. Reports give your accountant the exact EMP201 figures, which takes them minutes. Direct submission is on the roadmap [●]. If auto-filing is your single deciding factor, I will tell you honestly Sage or SimplePay fits better; for most businesses your size it is not."

## Timing and Inertia

**"We're mid tax year; we'll look in March."**
> "March is the busiest possible time to switch: new tables, EMP501 prep, everyone rushed. Switching now means the parallel run happens in a calm month and you enter March already settled. Also, the trial costs nothing today; the March version of you will be grateful."

**"Too busy to migrate."**
> "The whole setup is about [10] hours of your admin's time spread over two weeks, with our checklist and, on your plan, a live session where we load employees together. Compare that with the hours payroll takes you every single month."

**"My accountant handles it and it works."**
> "Keep the accountant, genuinely: they should review your first run and keep filing your returns. NovaHR replaces the spreadsheet-and-email machinery underneath, and gives them cleaner numbers. Many accountants prefer clients on it; want me to speak with yours directly?"

## Product Gaps

**"You don't have [feature]."**
> If roadmap: "It is on the roadmap [timing if committed]. What would you need it to do exactly? That shapes how we build it." If not planned: "We do not, and I will not pretend otherwise. Is it a dealbreaker or a nice-to-have?" (Log it in the feature register either way.)

## The Silent Objection

No reply after a proposal usually means: no internal sponsor, price shock unvoiced, or fear of migration effort. Use the gone-quiet template (outreach-templates.md) and ask directly which it is; naming it is not rude, it is a relief.
