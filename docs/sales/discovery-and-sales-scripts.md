# NovaHR Discovery Call Questions and Sales Scripts

**Audience:** internal (founder-led sales). Calls are 25-30 minutes: 15 discovery, 10 targeted demo or next-step, 5 close.

---

## 1. Call Opening (60 seconds)

> "Thanks for the time, [name]. The way I run these: I ask about how you handle HR and payroll today, and if it looks like NovaHR genuinely helps, I show you exactly the parts that matter to you, and if it does not fit, I will say so. Fair?"

(Permission-based, sets honesty frame, takes pressure off.)

## 2. Discovery Questions

### Situation (pick 3-4)
1. "How many people are on payroll right now, and is that growing?"
2. "Walk me through last month's payroll: who does what, from timesheets to payslips in hands?"
3. "What are you using today: bureau, accountant, Sage, spreadsheets?"
4. "How do employees request leave, and how do you track balances?"
5. "Who looks after EMP201s and the EMP501 reconciliation?"

### Pain (pick 2-3; go deeper on any flinch)
6. "What part of that process eats the most time?"
7. "Have you ever had a payslip error or a SARS penalty? What happened?"
8. "If [payroll person] was off sick on the 24th, what happens?"
9. "Where do employee ID numbers and salaries live right now? Who can open that file?" (POPIA angle)
10. "What made you take this call now?" (the trigger event: always ask)

### Impact
11. "What does the bureau/accountant cost per month?" / "How many hours does payroll take you?"
12. "What would you do with that day back every month?"

### Fit and process
13. "Anything unusual in your payroll: commissions, shift overtime, multiple pay frequencies?"
14. "If this works, who besides you decides, and what would they need to see?"
15. "When would you ideally run your first payroll on a new system?" (anchor: month-start switch is cleanest)

## 3. Demo Guidance

Show only what maps to their stated pain, in this order of impact:

1. Their scenario: add an employee like theirs, run a payslip, show PAYE/UIF calculated;
2. The payslip PDF and employee self-service ("your staff stop asking you for payslips");
3. Leave request and approval on a phone;
4. The report their accountant needs for EMP201.

Never demo settings screens, edge features, or anything they did not mention.

## 4. Closing Scripts

**Trial close:** "Based on what you have seen, does this solve the [specific pain they named]?"

**Direct close (they are warm):**
> "Here is what I suggest: start the free trial today, I will send the setup guide, and we book 30 minutes next week to load your first employees together. If the parallel run against your current payroll does not tie out, you have lost nothing. Want me to set that up?"

**Price question:**
> "For [their employee count] employees you are on the [plan] at [insert plan price] a month, flat, no per-payslip fees, no setup fee. You told me the bureau is costing about [their current cost] plus your time, so you would be saving roughly [insert plan price] a month. And there is a 14-day money-back guarantee."

**Stall ("send me info"):**
> "Happy to. So I send the right thing: is it the pricing you want to double-check, how the payroll works, or do you need to bring [other decision maker] in? ... Great, I will send the one-pager plus a proposal with your numbers, and pencil a 15-minute call for [day] to answer questions. If it is a no by then, just tell me, that is a fine answer."

## 5. Disqualify Fast (and kindly)

Say no when: they want a full-service bureau; they need features we lack for genuinely complex payroll (expat, share schemes) today; they are under 3 employees with a free accountant arrangement and no HR pain. Script:

> "Honestly, I do not think we are your best option for [reason]: [alternative] fits better. When [condition changes], come back to me."

(Referrals from honest disqualifications are real.)

## 6. After Every Call

Log in CRM within 10 minutes: headcount, current tool and cost, named pains (verbatim), trigger event, decision process, next step with a date. No next step with a date means the deal does not exist.
