Five test companies

Create all five by self serve signup with different emails you control. Companies A, B and C are the main coverage set: they are configured so that mutually exclusive settings (SDL liable or not, pension or none, accrual or upfront leave, paid or statutory maternity, one branch or many, one pay frequency or several, Netcash or manual EFT) are each exercised at least once. Companies D and E are deliberately small and strange: a one person company and a grab bag of boundary employees (65+ and 75+ rebates, top tax bracket, below the tax threshold, exactly on the UIF ceiling, weekly unpaid leave, negative net). Their job is to break happy path assumptions, not to look realistic.

Scenario inputs (use these exact amounts)

  • Variable pay (Company A, Aisha, monthly run): Overtime 10 hours at R120/hour (expect R1 800 = 10 × 120 × 1.5), Commission R2 500, Bonus R5 000 (annual payment method), post-tax deduction R300.
  • Recurring component: Pieter, permanent Commission R2 000/month.
  • Back pay / arrears: Nomsa, monthly shortfall R2 000 × 3 months = R6 000.
  • Leave encashment: Aisha, encash 2 annual days.
  • Unpaid leave (Company B): Lerato, 3 unpaid working days inside the current month (expect a pro-rata reduction and lower PAYE).
  • Public holiday: add a custom holiday named "Company founders day" on any weekday next month.
  • UIF checks: Nomsa, Pieter, Aisha earn above the R17 712 ceiling so employee UIF should be exactly R177.12; Lerato R7 500 → R75.00; Kabelo R4 500 → R45.00.
  • SDL check: Company A (over R500k/yr) charges SDL 1%; Company B (under R500k) charges no SDL.
