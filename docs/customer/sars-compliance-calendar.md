# SARS and Labour Compliance Calendar for Employers

**Provided by NovaHR as a reference. Verify current-year dates on sars.gov.za and labour.gov.za; deadlines falling on weekends/holidays generally move to the prior business day for payments.**

---

## Monthly (every month)

| Deadline | Obligation | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **7th** | **EMP201** return and payment | PAYE + UIF + SDL for the previous month, via eFiling/EFT. Late payment: 10% penalty plus interest, automatic |
| **7th** | UIF payment | Included in EMP201 if SARS-registered; otherwise direct to the UIF |
| Month-end | **UI-19 declaration** | Employee and contribution declarations to the Department of Employment and Labour (uFiling), including new starters and terminations |

## Annual and Bi-Annual

| Period | Obligation | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| **March** | New tax year begins (1 March) | New SARS tables apply from the first March payroll. NovaHR ships the update; verify your first March run |
| **April/May** | **EMP501 annual reconciliation** | Reconcile 12 months of EMP201s against payroll; SARS opens the window by notice (historically 1 April to 31 May) |
| **After annual EMP501** | **IRP5/IT3(a) certificates** | Issue to all employees for their income tax returns |
| **September/October** | **EMP501 interim reconciliation** | Covers March to August; window per annual SARS notice |
| **April-June** | **Workplace Skills Plan / Annual Training Report** | To your SETA (30 April deadline typically) to claim SDL mandatory grants back |
| **Annually** | **Return of Earnings (ROE / W.As.8)** | COIDA return to the Compensation Fund; window announced yearly (typically opens 1 April) |
| **Employment Equity reporting** | 1 September to 15 January (online) | Designated employers only (50+ employees or sector turnover thresholds) |

## Event-Driven

| Event | Obligation |
|---|---|
| New employee | Capture tax number; declare on next UI-19; register for PAYE if first employee |
| Termination | UI-19 update (enables UIF benefit claims); final payslip must include accrued leave payout |
| Crossing R500,000 annual payroll | Register for SDL |
| Salary threshold changes gazetted | Re-check BCEA earnings threshold impact on overtime rights |

## Penalties for Missing Deadlines

- EMP201 late: 10% of the amount, automatic, plus interest;
- EMP501 late or incorrect: administrative penalties per certificate;
- UI-19 failures: employees cannot claim benefits, and non-compliance is an offence;
- ROE late: penalties and inability to obtain a Letter of Good Standing (needed for many contracts).
