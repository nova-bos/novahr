# NovaHR Onboarding Guide

**Audience:** New customers (HR Administrators and business owners)
**Timeline:** Designed as a 2-week path from sign-up to first live payroll.

---

## How Onboarding Works

| Phase | Days | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Foundation | 1-2 | Company configured, structure created |
| 2. Data | 3-7 | All employees captured and verified |
| 3. Verification | 8-11 | Test payroll run checked against your current payroll |
| 4. Go-live | 12-14 | First live run approved and published |

Growth and Scale plans include a live onboarding session; book it via hello@novahr.co.za.

## Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-2)

- [ ] Accept the Terms of Service and Data Processing Agreement (account owner);
- [ ] Complete company profile (Settings, Company);
- [ ] Capture PAYE, UIF, SDL reference numbers (Settings, Payroll);
- [ ] Confirm SDL liability (annual payroll above R500,000?);
- [ ] Set pay frequency and pay day;
- [ ] Configure leave policies (defaults are BCEA minimums; align to your contracts);
- [ ] Create departments and positions.

## Phase 2: Data (Days 3-7)

- [ ] Gather source data (use the Payroll Setup Guide checklist);
- [ ] Capture every active employee: identity, employment, pay, banking, leave balances;
- [ ] Verify the three fields that most often cause payslip errors:
  - Date of birth (drives age rebates),
  - Medical aid dependant count (drives tax credits),
  - Travel allowance inclusion rate (80% default; 20% only with logbook substantiation);
- [ ] Set opening leave balances as at your cut-over date;
- [ ] Invite managers and employees; assign roles.

## Phase 3: Verification (Days 8-11)

- [ ] Create a test payroll run for the current period;
- [ ] **Parallel run:** compare every employee's PAYE, UIF, and net pay against your current payroll system or accountant's calculation;
- [ ] Investigate every difference: most trace to dependant counts, pension percentages, or allowance treatment;
- [ ] Have your accountant or tax practitioner sign off one sample payslip;
- [ ] Read and acknowledge the Compliance Disclaimer and Customer Responsibilities;
- [ ] Confirm payslip email delivery (send yourself a test).

## Phase 4: Go-Live (Days 12-14)

- [ ] Final data check: joiners, leavers, once-off items for the period;
- [ ] Run, review, and approve payroll;
- [ ] Publish payslips (employees are notified);
- [ ] Export the run report and archive it (SARS requires 5-year retention);
- [ ] Diarise statutory deadlines: EMP201 by the 7th, UI-19 monthly (see the SARS Compliance Calendar).

## After Go-Live

- Month 2: run payroll without the parallel check but sample-verify 2-3 payslips;
- Review the audit log periodically (Settings, Audit Log);
- Send feedback and feature requests to hello@novahr.co.za;
- Every March: SARS updates tax tables; NovaHR ships the update and notifies you; verify your first March run.

## Common Onboarding Pitfalls

1. **Wrong opening leave balances:** anchor to the cut-over date, not the calendar year;
2. **Missing income tax numbers:** capture them now, you need them for IRP5s;
3. **Medical aid captured as a deduction but dependants not set:** the deduction reduces net pay, dependants reduce tax; both matter;
4. **Publishing before verifying:** employees see published payslips immediately;
5. **Not exporting before cancelling an old system:** you must retain 5 years of history from your previous provider.
