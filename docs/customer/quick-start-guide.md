# NovaHR Quick Start Guide

**Audience:** New HR Administrators
**Time required:** About 30 minutes
**Goal:** From empty account to your first calculated payslip.

---

## Before You Start

Have these at hand:

- Company details: registered name, address, PAYE / UIF / SDL reference numbers (from SARS and the Department of Employment and Labour);
- For each employee: full name, SA ID number, income tax number, date of birth, bank details, salary and allowances, pension and medical aid details, start date, leave balances.

## Step 1: Set Up Your Company (5 min)

1. Log in and open **Settings, Company**.
2. Complete the company profile: legal name, address, contact details.
3. Open **Settings, Payroll** and capture your PAYE, UIF, and SDL reference numbers, and confirm whether your company is SDL-liable (annual payroll above R500,000).
4. Set your pay day and pay frequency.

## Step 2: Create Structure (5 min)

1. **Departments:** add your departments (e.g. Operations, Sales, Admin).
2. **Positions:** add job titles and link them to departments.

## Step 3: Add Employees (10 min)

1. Open **Employees, Add Employee**.
2. Capture personal details (ID number, date of birth, contact details), employment details (position, department, start date), and pay details (salary, allowances, pension %, medical aid dependants, bank account).
3. Set opening leave balances so accruals start from the right point.
4. Repeat for each employee. Double-check dates of birth (they drive tax rebates) and medical dependants (they drive tax credits).

## Step 4: Invite Users (3 min)

1. Open **Settings, Users** and invite your employees and managers by email.
2. Assign roles: Employee (self-service), Manager (team approvals), HR Admin (full access), Executive (dashboards).
3. Invitees receive an email link to set their own password.

## Step 5: Run a Test Payroll (5 min)

1. Open **Payroll, New Run** and select the current period.
2. Review each employee's calculated payslip: gross, PAYE, UIF, deductions, net.
3. Cross-check one payslip against your previous payroll or the SARS calculator.
4. **Do not publish yet** if you are still verifying: published payslips are visible to employees.

## Step 6: Go Live

1. Work through the Onboarding Guide's go-live checklist (parallel run recommended for your first month);
2. Approve and publish the run; employees are notified and can download payslip PDFs;
3. Use **Reports** for your EMP201 figures (due to SARS by the 7th of next month).
