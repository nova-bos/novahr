## 1. Public Holidays: Legal Basis

The Public Holidays Act 36 of 1994 prescribes 12 public holidays. When a public holiday falls on a **Sunday, the following Monday** is a public holiday. Government may proclaim additional once-off holidays.

### South African public holidays

| Date | Holiday |
|---|---|
| 1 January | New Year's Day |
| 21 March | Human Rights Day |
| Friday before Easter Sunday | Good Friday (movable) |
| Monday after Easter Sunday | Family Day (movable) |
| 27 April | Freedom Day |
| 1 May | Workers' Day |
| 16 June | Youth Day |
| 9 August | National Women's Day |
| 24 September | Heritage Day |
| 16 December | Day of Reconciliation |
| 25 December | Christmas Day |
| 26 December | Day of Goodwill |

NovaHR maintains the holiday calendar per year, including Sunday-shift Mondays and gazetted once-off holidays. Leave requests spanning public holidays do not deduct the holiday from annual leave balances. Attendance marks public holidays distinctly.

### Pay rules (BCEA s 18)

- An employee who does **not work** on a public holiday that falls on a working day receives normal pay for the day;
- An employee who **works** on a public holiday must be paid at least **double the daily wage**, or, if the holiday falls on a day the employee would not ordinarily work, ordinary daily wage plus pay for time worked;
- Work on public holidays is by agreement only.

## 2. Overtime: Legal Basis (BCEA ss 9-10)

Applies to employees within the BCEA working-time chapter (it does not apply to, among others, senior managerial employees or employees earning above the Minister's earnings threshold, currently gazetted from time to time [verify current threshold ●]).

| Rule | Minimum |
|---|---|
| Ordinary hours | Max 45 per week (9 per day for 5-day week; 8 for 6-day week) |
| Overtime | By agreement only; max 10 hours per week (extendable to 15 by collective agreement for up to 2 months a year) |
| Overtime rate | At least **1.5x** the ordinary wage; or paid time off by agreement (90 minutes off per overtime hour) |
| Sunday work (s 16) | **2x** ordinary wage, or 1.5x if the employee ordinarily works Sundays; minimum shift protections apply |
| Night work (s 17) | By agreement, with an allowance or reduced hours, and transport available |

## 3. How NovaHR Handles Overtime

- Overtime hours are captured per employee per period (manually or from attendance);
- Rates default to 1.5x ordinary, 2x for Sundays and public holidays, computed from the employee's derived hourly rate;
- Overtime appears as a separate earning line on the payslip with hours and rate, satisfying BCEA s 33 disclosure;
- Overtime is taxable remuneration and flows into PAYE and UIF calculations;
- The employer is responsible for ensuring overtime was agreed and within statutory caps; NovaHR records and pays what is captured, and flags weeks exceeding 10 overtime hours [flagging: roadmap ●].

## 4. Employer Checklist

- [ ] Overtime agreements in employment contracts for affected staff;
- [ ] Earnings-threshold employees identified (overtime chapter does not apply above the threshold);
- [ ] Correct working patterns per employee (drives daily wage derivation);
- [ ] Public holiday work agreed in advance and captured at the correct multiple;
- [ ] Attendance records retained (BCEA s 31: 3 years).
